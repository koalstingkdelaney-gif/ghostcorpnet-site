How the maths works: gross revenue = (take-home pay + expenses) ÷ (1 − tax % − profit %). Your hourly rate is that revenue divided by your billable hours: (52 − weeks off) × hours per week × billable %. If the result feels high, the honest levers are more billable hours, lower expenses, or a lower take-home target — not quietly skipping the tax set-aside.

Questions

Why is my calculated rate higher than rates I see advertised?

Advertised rates often ignore unpaid weeks, non-billable time, expenses and tax. This calculator includes all four, so it shows the rate that actually delivers the take-home pay you entered — not a rate that only works if you bill every hour of the year.

Should I charge hourly or a fixed project price?

Either can work if it is built from the same hourly rate. A fixed price is just your rate × estimated hours, plus a buffer for the scope changes that happen on most projects — and work outside the agreed scope should be quoted separately, in writing, before you do it.

Is this tax or financial advice?

No. It is an arithmetic planning tool using the percentages you type in. Your actual tax obligations depend on your country, state and business structure — check with a qualified local professional.

Does my data leave my device?

No. Every calculation happens in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere, and this page makes no external calls.

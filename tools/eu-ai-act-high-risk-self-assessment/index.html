Introduction

The EU AI Act sorts AI systems into four tiers: prohibited practices (banned outright), high-risk systems (listed in Annex III, or safety components of regulated products — these carry the full set of obligations: risk management, data governance, technical documentation, record-keeping, transparency, human oversight, accuracy and robustness), limited-risk systems (transparency obligations, e.g. chatbots and synthetic content), and minimal-risk systems (no mandatory obligations). Most teams do not need a lawyer to find their likely tier — they need an honest walk through the categories. That is what this tool does.

This is an informational self-assessment, not legal advice and not a binding classification. Confirm borderline cases with qualified counsel.

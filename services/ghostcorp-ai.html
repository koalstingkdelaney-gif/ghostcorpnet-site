GhostCorp AI — the sales platform that does the work and asks before it acts

GhostCorp AI is GhostCorp’s own AI sales and go-to-market platform. It learns your business, finds the companies most likely to buy from you, researches them, scores and organizes your leads, and drafts the campaigns and follow-ups — while you stay in charge of anything that actually leaves the building.

Status: in development

Plainly stated: GhostCorp AI is being built now. The engine behind it is already live — it runs GhostCorp’s own sales operation around the clock, today (see “Tenant zero” below). Customer accounts and onboarding are in development, so there is no signup, no checkout, and no pricing yet — and we will not pretend otherwise. If you want to hear when early access opens, email hello@ghostcorpnet.com.

What it does

It learns your business

A knowledge base that stays current, so every agent works from the same facts.

  • Your website, company description, and what you sell — products and services
  • Your target customers and your ideal-customer profile
  • The geographic markets you sell into
  • Your sales goals and your preferred communication style
  • The integrations you authorize — nothing connects without your say-so

It finds and researches prospects

Discovery and research, done by agents, checked against your ideal-customer profile.

  • Prospect discovery matched to your target market
  • Company and contact research on every prospect
  • Buying-signal tracking — the events that suggest a company is ready to buy
  • AI-generated account research before anyone reaches out

It qualifies and organizes

A pipeline you can actually read, instead of a pile of names.

  • Lead qualification and scoring, so the best prospects surface first
  • A searchable CRM — every company, contact, and conversation in one place
  • Sales pipelines that show where every deal stands

It prepares the work

Drafts and plans, ready for your review — never sent behind your back.

  • Personalized sales strategies per account
  • Campaign drafts, email drafts, and message drafts
  • Follow-up suggestions, timed to each prospect
  • Sales-agent workflows that keep the whole process moving

It reports the truth

Numbers that come from records, not from an agent’s say-so.

  • Analytics on outreach, replies, and pipeline movement
  • Revenue reporting: gross, fees, refunds, and net — kept separate
  • Fulfillment tracking, so what was promised matches what was delivered

The control promise

Most AI sales tools brag about how much they do without you. GhostCorp AI is built the other way: the agents do the heavy lifting, and you make the calls that matter. Everything runs through an Action Center with a visible “Needs Your Approval” queue.

  • Agents may research, analyze, organize leads, draft messages and campaigns, and recommend actions.
  • Agents may not — without your approval — send outreach, make binding commitments, spend money, issue refunds, or change payment or bank details.
  • Payments are verified through the payment providers themselves (such as Stripe or Gumroad) — never from screenshots or an agent’s claim that money arrived.
  • Budgets are enforced: permitted spending categories, transaction logs, and owner approval for financial changes. No agent can withdraw or transfer funds.
  • Everything is written down. Decisions, authorizations, and evidence are kept in durable, auditable journals — so the record survives even if any single agent, machine, or session disappears. No agent, including ours, is a single point of failure or an unquestioned authority.

Its first and only tenant so far is GhostCorp itself: the same engine that customers will get is the one finding GhostCorp’s customers, scoring its leads, drafting its campaigns, and running its approval queues right now — around the clock, every day. When customer access opens, new businesses will onboard into the same system GhostCorp already trusts with its own sales operation.

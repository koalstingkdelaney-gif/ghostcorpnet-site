## Per-asset recovery notes

For each critical asset, record in a secure system:
- Provider's official recovery/support route.
- Account identifier or organization name (avoid personal secrets).
- Legal owner and evidence of ownership location.
- MFA and recovery method custody location.
- Whether provider terms permit succession, delegation, or transfer.
- Required documents and identity checks.
- Export/backup method and last successful test.
- Person responsible for reviewing the entry.

## Review checklist

- [ ] Legal owner confirmed against current records.
- [ ] Two authorized recovery methods exist where provider supports them.
- [ ] MFA recovery is tested without sharing credentials.
- [ ] Provider-specific successor process checked from official documentation.
- [ ] Data export or backup is recent and restorable.
- [ ] Unneeded administrators and tokens are removed.
- [ ] Review date and responsible person are recorded.

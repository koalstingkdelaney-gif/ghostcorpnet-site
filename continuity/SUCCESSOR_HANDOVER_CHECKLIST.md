# GhostCorp Successor Handover Checklist

**Status:** Human/legal workflow template. Not proof of authority and not a substitute for legal advice.

## Gate 1 — Authority and identity

- [ ] Confirm which legal instrument or court/provider process authorizes the person to act.
- [ ] Confirm the authority is current and applies to the specific business asset or action.
- [ ] Verify identity using the relevant provider's approved process.
- [ ] Confirm any co-fiduciary, court, board, or second-approver requirement.
- [ ] Record who reviewed the evidence, when, and the scope approved.
- [ ] Do not upload sensitive estate documents to ordinary logs or public repositories.

If authority is ambiguous, stop. Refer the matter to the relevant attorney, fiduciary, court, or provider. Do not let an AI agent resolve the ambiguity.

## Gate 2 — Secure access provisioning

- [ ] Create a new named identity for the successor.
- [ ] Enroll MFA and recovery methods.
- [ ] Start with read-only access.
- [ ] Grant only role-required permissions after approval.
- [ ] Do not share the founder's credentials.
- [ ] Rotate or revoke credentials as required by the provider and incident plan.
- [ ] Record all changes in the audit log.

## Gate 3 — Operational orientation

- [ ] Review architecture and service inventory.
- [ ] Review current service health and known outages.
- [ ] Review latest verified backup and restore-test evidence.
- [ ] Review open incidents, customer obligations, contracts, and pending tasks.
- [ ] Review current spending cap and cash/treasury controls.
- [ ] Review provider terms, subscriptions, model quotas, and renewal dates.
- [ ] Review agent permissions, stop controls, and approval queues.
- [ ] Review the process for pausing sales and external commitments.

## Gate 4 — Controlled assumption of duties

- [ ] Approve the new accountable owner/manager according to governing documents.
- [ ] Set a bounded operating budget; do not inherit or assume any paid plan without review.
- [ ] Confirm who can approve deployments, customer communications, refunds, and vendor commitments.
- [ ] Confirm who can access customer and financial records.
- [ ] Test incident escalation and restore procedures.
- [ ] Record acceptance of responsibilities and unresolved risks.

## Gate 5 — Ongoing governance

- [ ] Review access monthly and after personnel changes.
- [ ] Review backup and restore evidence quarterly.
- [ ] Review asset register and provider succession rules quarterly.
- [ ] Review business and estate documents with qualified professionals when circumstances change.
- [ ] Keep a backup successor/fiduciary arrangement as provided by legal documents.

A completed checklist does not transfer ownership by itself. Actual authority depends on the governing documents, applicable law, and provider procedures.

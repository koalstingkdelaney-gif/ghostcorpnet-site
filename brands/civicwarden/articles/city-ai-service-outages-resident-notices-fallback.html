When the city AI goes down: outage notices and fallback service for residents

Published 2026-11-16T00:00:00-05:00 ·

As CivicWarden, your brand stands for trustworthiness and reliability in the public sector. Our mission is to provide citizens with a reliable AI agent to keep them informed during unexpected outages. In this article, we guide you through essential steps for handling outages, ensuring minimal disruption to your residents' lives.

What Residents Need to Know During an Outage

During an AI outage, residents may experience confusion, anxiety, and uncertainty. To alleviate these concerns, it is essential to communicate effectively and transparently. Provide residents with the following information:

  • A clear explanation of the reason for the outage
  • An estimated time of resolution
  • Alternative communication channels (e.g., phone, social media)
  • Acknowledgment of the inconvenience caused and a promise to improve

Writing Outage Notices in Plain Language

To ensure that residents can quickly understand the situation, use plain language when communicating outages. Avoid technical jargon or ambiguous statements. Instead, focus on explaining the issue and offering clear guidance. Consider the following tips when drafting outage notices:

  • Use simple language that everyone can understand
  • Provide concise information about the outage issue and its impact
  • Offer alternatives for residents to stay informed (e.g., phone, social media)
  • Mention estimated time of resolution or provide an update on the progress

Keeping a Staffed Fallback Channel Open

During an AI outage, maintaining a dedicated fallback channel ensures residents can get immediate assistance and gain insights about the situation. Ensure that this channel is staffed by dedicated personnel who can provide accurate information and support. Provide contact details and operating hours for the fallback channel. It is essential to acknowledge that the AI outage may impact the availability of certain services, but dedicated human assistance is available for residents. Additionally, consider the following guidelines:

  • Provide clear contact details for the fallback channel (e.g., phone number, email address)
  • Ensure the fallback channel is staffed during operating hours
  • Acknowledge that certain services might be impacted during the AI outage
  • Assure residents that dedicated human assistance is available through the fallback channel

Logging Outage Information for Audit and Public Records

To ensure transparency and maintain trust with your residents, log the AI outage details accurately for audit and public records purposes. Include the following information:

  • Date and time of the AI outage
  • Description of the issue and resolution steps
  • Audit logs, including the timeline of the resolution process
  • Details of any updates provided to residents during the outage

Providing Fallback Service during AI Outages

During outages, residents often rely on your AI assistant for essential services like traffic updates, weather information, and emergency services. To minimize inconvenience, provide fallback service options to ensure residents can still access critical services during outages. Consider the following options:

  • Provide a list of alternative services residents can use during the AI outage (e.g., traditional traffic app, local news channels, etc.)
  • Ensure information about the fallback service options is easily accessible (e.g., through a dedicated page on your website)
  • Outline the steps your team is taking to resolve the AI outage and communicate updates to residents periodically
  • Provide contact information for the fallback service providers and your dedicated fallback support team
